## Supplementary bilingual findings

The [focused bilingual assessment](../simulation/bilingual_assessment/results.md) added local mass, ion, neutrality, retained-vocabulary and shared-science concerns. IA-D02 language support and IA-D03 explanations remain Partial. A bilingual field, permitted route or unchanged profile is not proof that a language difficulty was resolved. Better later support also does not correct the earlier stored definition. No original argument or output score was upgraded.

## Scholarly warrants and limits

TTF motivates task alignment, not measured learner fit. Scaffolding theory supplies contingency/fading benchmarks, not demonstrated performance-calibrated support. Selective-term research comes from scientific-paper translation, not Burmese beginners. Controlled evaluation does not establish everyday learner utility. Bilingual error-analysis literature motivates contextual inspection, not a professional MQM score.
