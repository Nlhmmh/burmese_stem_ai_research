## Scholarly warrants and new evidence

[Hevner et al. (2004, p. 86, Table 2)](https://doi.org/10.2307/25148625)
support literature-grounded informed argument as a complementary evaluation
method. [Goodhue and Thompson (1995)](https://doi.org/10.2307/249689) provide
the existing task–technology fit warrant. Applying it here means that a
displayed bilingual field must be assessed against the terminology task, not
treated as evidence of task success merely because it exists.

[Kleidermacher and Zou (2026)](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.findings-eacl.204)
provide the existing rationale for selective technical-term retention. Their
scientific-paper translation setting does not certify Burmese novice support.
The annotation distinguishes useful retained identifiers from unglossed
explanatory vocabulary and unintended third-language word fragments.

[Van de Pol et al. (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9127-6)
provide the existing scaffolding benchmark. A permitted adaptation route is
not evidence that the revised wording addresses the reported difficulty.
[Freitag et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1162/tacl_a_00437) supply an additional
methodological warrant for examining explicit contextual errors. Their
professional evaluation is not reproduced by these model-assisted annotations.

The predeclared purposeful sample includes 32 of 91 delivered outputs and
104 paired passages. It contains all three language-help adaptations, all
delivered main concepts through B-path initial outputs and selected risk-focused
adaptations. There are four local Major annotations in three outputs,
27 Minor annotations and ten shared-content advisories. These counts are not
weighted MQM scores, representative prevalence or new output Pass/Fail totals.

## Counterevidence, dependency and chronology

BIL-F07 because the neutral assertion is negated or seriously obscured.
The correct target hint and different units/rules must be retained when
explaining the discrepancy. The two gravity passages are one failed output,
not two failed simulations. The ion failure remains one original output.

The new annotations reuse the same simulation text and known earlier findings.
They were produced by the AI assistant, not an independent qualified human
assessor. Prior author acceptance of the earlier reviews does not establish
acceptance of this new addendum. Agreement is dependent evidence. The
historical conceptual argument/triangulation, original criteria and technical
baseline are not retrospectively rewritten.
