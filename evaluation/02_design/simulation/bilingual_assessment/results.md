# Supplementary bilingual error assessment

This review examined **32 saved outputs and 104 English–Burmese passage pairs**. Outputs were selected using earlier concerns, not randomly. All passages in each selected output were checked. Initial outputs have five passages; adaptations have one.

Open **Show all pairs** in the **Complete English–Burmese pairs** column to read the full saved text, including passages with no flagged issue. Compare it with the findings and suggested revisions in the same row.

**AI first, human review afterwards.** AI prepared the initial error annotations, checked source references and proposed wording changes. A human reviewer then checked the annotations against the original English/Burmese text and scientific references, reviewed the severity and suggested changes, and confirmed the final findings. This checking was confirmed in [the shared protocol](../../../00_protocol/evaluation_protocol.md#human-verification-and-ai-assistance). The table below presents the human-confirmed assessment, not AI findings accepted without review.

**How to read the table.** Major means meaning is wrong, reversed or seriously unclear. Minor means meaning can still be understood but wording needs improvement. Advisory means a concern shared by both languages, not a confirmed translation error. The 41 findings comprise **4 Major, 27 Minor and 10 Advisory**. English was checked for scientific accuracy too. Local categories were informed by [Freitag et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1162/tacl_a_00437) and [the MQM Core Typology](https://www.themqm.org/mqm-pillars/the-mqm-core-typology/), but no standard MQM score is claimed.
