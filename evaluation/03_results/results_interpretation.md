## Retained disagreements and limitations

The original pH whole-output Partial and supplementary Major passage concern use different units/rules and are both retained. Better later adaptations do not repair earlier stored errors. Safe rejection with unchanged storage is not successful delivery. BB22's public anonymous-identity behavior does not meet the original missing-identity oracle, despite no observed foreign access. The generated ambiguity clarification consumed a round; the failed supplemental assumption remains visible.

The exact timeout ceiling was not demonstrated by live cancellation at 52.825 seconds. The two-round storage bound does not prevent duplicate provider work. Preference-save infrastructure recovery, semantic novelty in two fixture cases and independent participant/expert evaluation were not assessed. No conclusion establishes achievement, retention, mastery, ideal support dose, lower cognitive load, barrier reduction or superiority.
