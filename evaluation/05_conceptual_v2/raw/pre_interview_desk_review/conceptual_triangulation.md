# Conceptual evaluation synthesis — iteration 2

**Baseline:** CA-V2-20261023

**Date:** 23 October 2026, Pacific/Auckland

**Objects:** The three supplied v2 conceptual artefacts.

## Overall result

The refined artefacts provide a clearer conceptual account of terminology support, core explanation and learner-responsive assistance. They distinguish core meaning from selectable aids, label learner response as self-reported support need, qualify the intended learning effects, and show returns to language support and core meaning.

The design remains supported with qualifications. Language-selection factors, interpretation of feedback, late intended-meaning correction and the conditions for reduced/paused support are not fully specified. The relationship model also contains “intended to supports”, which needs a wording correction. These findings favour focused clarification while retaining the three artefacts and seven responsibilities.

This is a new conceptual evaluation episode, not a rewrite of the [original evaluation](../01_conceptual/conceptual_triangulation.md). It assesses the [frozen v2 images](baseline/artefact_register.md), using four method records:

## Iteration result and implications

The [18-decision review](refinement_review.md) identifies four addressed changes, four partly addressed changes, nine retained scope decisions and one continuing claim boundary. Additional intended-effect qualifiers in CA2 improve the relationship model. These are categories of conceptual change, not efficacy scores.

The remaining actions are specific: correct grammar, clarify language-selection factors, state feedback interpretation, specify the late-correction exception, clarify pause/review boundaries and improve the crowded subtitle. The evaluated images remain unchanged, so these proposed actions cannot be reported as completed refinements.

No current finding requires adding a quiz, full proficiency profile, an eighth core stage or a separate verification stage. Content reliability still requires separate assessment; preserving scope does not dismiss that risk.

## What this evaluation establishes

The records establish an inspectable assessment of the frozen v2 representations: what they explicitly include, how they relate to requirements and literature, which constructed routes they can represent, and which earlier concerns remain. They provide a basis for presenting v2 as a **refined, conceptually evaluated proposal with stated limitations**.

They do not establish implementation correctness, reliable terminology extraction, valid learner diagnosis, appropriate Burmese wording, accurate generated science, actual task fit, learning improvement, retention, calibrated fading or transfer. No new system tests, independent expert interview or participant experiment were conducted for this episode.

For final reporting, distinguish original conceptual evaluation, refinement, this second conceptual evaluation and the separately recorded system evaluation. Do not backdate current findings into the first iteration or replace system outcomes with diagram judgements.
