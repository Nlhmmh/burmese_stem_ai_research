## Scholarly warrants

The substantive facts taken from the targeted primary checks are deliberately limited. Van de Pol et al. identifies contingency, fading and transfer as key scaffolding characteristics, while discussing diagnostic and measurement challenges. The current framework is evaluated against that benchmark, not assumed to reproduce human teaching. Kleidermacher and Zou supplies a reason to avoid automatically translating every familiar technical term. Kuzu's guided multilingual context limits direct transfer to self-directed support. Dunlosky and Rawson motivates caution about the accuracy of self-evaluation. Ji et al. motivates separating fluent generation from factual reliability. The particular stage boundaries and route recommendations in this evaluation are reasoned applications of these warrants.
