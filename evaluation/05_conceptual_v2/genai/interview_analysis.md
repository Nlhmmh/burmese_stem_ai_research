## Method

The fixed questions continued the first iteration's nine topics: coverage, coherence, theory, completeness, decisions, scenario applicability, risks, refinements and final judgement. They were asked sequentially in one conversation. The three images and a detailed transcription were supplied together. The final acknowledgement confirmed readability before the first question.

Responses were coded for support, concern, limitation or proposed refinement. The table below records the source questions, conceptual claim, linked combined finding and interpretation. Importance describes the consequence for the design or its interpretation; it is not a measured probability. Native judgements and confidence wording are retained separately.

A fresh conversation provides a separate response record, but not an independent human assessment or empirical replication. The fixed context frames the task, theory summaries simplify their sources, and the nine answers share one conversational history. Later answers can repeat earlier claims. The transcription error discussed below is an additional source of possible anchoring.

## Adjudication and implications

**Transcription correction.** The acknowledgement identified “indented to supports” in CA2. Direct reinspection of the frozen image confirms that wording. The supplied context instead asserted “intended to supports”, and Q7/Q8 later repeated it. Both raw versions remain preserved. The current analytical register and D01 now require correcting the image to “intended to support”. The model's later repetition is not treated as independent confirmation of the wrong transcription.

**Stage 4/5 boundary.** The interview continues to seek clearer boundaries even though the images now name core meaning and selectable aids. The existing R01 decision remains addressed at the label level, with a continuing explanation that content can overlap. No interview finding establishes a need to merge the responsibilities.

**Reliability.** A conceptual assurance concern is not an observed content failure. The interview does not validate scientific or Burmese output. Its concern is retained as a limitation and a reason for separate appraisal, not automatically converted into an eighth stage or a content-verification guarantee.

**C2 judgement.** Q9 uses Supported with qualification. The combined synthesis retains Partly supported because language/route selection, late context correction and pause conditions are material unresolved decisions under the protocol. The native interview rating remains visible; it is not overwritten or averaged.

**C4 judgement.** The interview did not receive source texts. The separate literature method can support the design rationale with transfer/access qualifications, while the interview's own C4 remains Not resolved. These are different evidence scopes.

**New dependency/participation concern.** GI16 extends the combined findings as V2-F15. D07 records optional clarification of “determines”, “leads to” and the scope of causal wording. A fixed arrow-label replacement is not presented as empirically optimal.
