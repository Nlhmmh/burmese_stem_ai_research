ion definition deepen earlier failures. The pH output was originally Partial
with unclear wording already noted. Its new passage-level Major severity does
not silently replace that rating. Twenty-seven Minor annotations and ten
shared-content advisories are separate measures. The latter are not counted
as translation errors.

technical simulation retains 55 attempts, 44 technical Pass, eight controlled
initial ambiguities and three technical Fail. The additional analysis uses
the same saved texts and cannot count as independent confirmation. Prior
author approval of earlier content review does not imply approval of the new
annotations. The supplementary assessment is model-assisted and exploratory,
not a newly completed human or independent expert review.

All 32 original appendix tables are preserved. Three new tables produce
35 appendix tables and five main tables. Table 4 has one additional row.
Six exact Assignment 4 keywords and two figures remain. The bibliography now
contains thirteen references. Original result and traceability CSVs are
