## Ion evidence recheck

The completed-run raw evidence for SIM08-B was inspected directly in
`evaluation/02_design/simulation/raw/SIM-RUN-01-results.jsonl`.
The initial returned response and persisted session have identical Burmese
and English technical explanations.

The Burmese sentence contains the full phrase `net charge မရှိတော့ဘဲ`,
not merely the suffix `မရှိတော့ဘဲ`. It says that net charge is no longer
present, then states that a positive or negative charge forms. This is an
internal contradiction. The corresponding English definition correctly says
that an ion acquires a nonzero net charge.

The paper now explains this contradiction precisely. It does not claim that
`မရှိတော့ဘဲ` is always wrong or that the output used the valid alternative
“no longer neutral”. No generated text, reference set, content rating or
simulation total was changed.
