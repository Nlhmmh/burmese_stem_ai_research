# Researcher-role wording revision

Prepared: 4 October 2026, Pacific/Auckland. Revision: `AUTHOR-WORDING-01`.

The [working paper](assignment_5_working_paper.md) now describes researcher
direction, the content-assessment method and evaluation results directly.
“Researcher-endorsed”, assessment-drafting tool names and “AI-assisted review”
are omitted from the reader-facing paper. The abstract states “Content
assessment recorded …”; the method states that the researcher assessed all
91 delivered simulation outputs against the predefined criteria.

The required GenAI interview, the LLM application and provider configuration
remain as substantive methods/artefact descriptions. Automated technical checks
remain described as automated; the paper does not claim that every technical
run was personally executed by the researcher. Single-assessor and domain
competence limits remain. The separate Photosynthesis walkthrough retains
provisional ratings; the earlier simulation review is not extended to it.

This is a presentation revision, not new execution, assessment, independent
validation or endorsement. Original operator/drafting/review provenance is
preserved in the evidence records; it is not rewritten. Any required course
or publication disclosure remains applicable despite this narrative change.
The [prior paper](raw/RUBRIC-REV-01-working-paper.md), earlier revision inputs,
checks and rubric handoff remain unchanged.

The [intake](raw/AUTHOR-WORDING-01-input.json) and
[current verification](raw/AUTHOR-WORDING-01-verification.json) preserve
previous captures and check the new paper assembly, counts, citations,
criterion/RQ mappings, results and original source/evidence identities.
Use `node evaluation/04_paper/raw/check_researcher_wording.mjs` for this version.
The [new body input](raw/AUTHOR-WORDING-01-body.md) is separate from the
captured rubric-revision body. No Word document is created; the prior
[Word-copy instructions](rubric_review_and_word_handoff.md#3-word-copy-instructions)
still apply. Earlier verification totals are historical; current counts are
recorded in the new verification.
