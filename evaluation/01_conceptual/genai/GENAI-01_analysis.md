# GenAI conceptual evaluation

## Method and records

GENAI-01 assessed the original seven-stage framework before refinement. Nine fixed questions were asked in a fresh Temporary Chat on 28 September 2026, 11:10–11:30 AM NZDT, without browsing or memory. GPT-5.6 Sol with High reasoning was displayed, but the provider version was not separately verified. Nay Lin Htet conducted the interview. The complete context prompt, questions and responses are preserved as GENAI-01_interview.md inside the original-document archive.

The supplied persona was “a critical evaluator of a conceptual artefact in Information Systems research”. The framework, PIRQOA, theories and photosynthesis scenario were supplied as text. The nine topics were coverage, coherence, theory, completeness/redundancy, decision control, scenario use, risks, scope-preserving improvements and final structured judgement. The critique was not a human expert interview.

Responses were coded as support, concern, missing element, unsupported assumption, proposed improvement or out of scope, then compared with literature, informed argument and scenario evidence.

| C5 — Scenario Applicability | Applicable with minor issues | All seven stages instantiated in Photosynthesis scenario | Self-report does not diagnose what should change | High |

The strongest findings were coverage of RQ1–RQ3, assistance beyond literal translation and an explicit feedback loop. Concerns were inaccurate self-report, insufficient adaptation/language-selection rules and unclear Stage 4/5 or feedback boundaries. The recommendation was clarification and moderate refinement, not a new eighth stage or wholesale redesign.

## Interpretation and limits

Confidence labels are the model's own ratings, not probabilities or external validation. Completeness and requirement coverage were different questions, so “fully covered” and “mostly complete” are retained rather than averaged. Original C4 concerned boundaries, not the later protocol's literature-consistency criterion. One model's critique supplies a challenge to assumptions, not evidence of learner fit, content correctness or improved learning.

The [conceptual synthesis](../conceptual_triangulation.md) records accepted and rejected refinements. Evidence E049–E050 identifies the historical interview and analysis versions; full originals remain archived.
