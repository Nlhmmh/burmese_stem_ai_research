## Combined findings

Four methods examined the original framework. They support terminology/context responsibility, explanation beyond translation and responsive assistance. Agreement sometimes reuses the same sources or scenario, so it is not independent replication. Native method ratings are retained in the individual reports, not averaged.

| Criterion | Current synthesis | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 Requirement coverage | fully supported for the stated responsibilities | Not proof that exactly seven stages are necessary |
| C2 Coherence | partially supported | Boundaries and support decisions required clarification |
| C3 Theory | partially supported | Task alignment and responsiveness are plausible, but fit, calibrated fading and transfer were not measured |
| C4 Literature | partially supported | Language/subject transfer and source-access limitations remain |
| C5 Scenario use | partially supported | One coherent illustration, with unspecified language and adaptation rationale |

## Refinement decisions

| Decision | Basis and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Retain seven responsibilities | No method established a need for an eighth stage or wholesale redesign |
| Separate core meaning from additional scaffolding | Clarify responsibilities without claiming that explanatory content never overlaps |
| Label responses as self-reported support need | Avoid objective-understanding or misconception-diagnosis claims |
| Clarify language selection | Preserve useful English terms with concept-scoped bilingual explanation |
| Permit bounded reconsideration | Language, core meaning or intended context can be revisited when the reported problem concerns them |
| Make route selection application-controlled | Later implementation decision, not an earlier conceptual finding |
| Use two persisted adaptations and explicit Finish | Engineering/scope rules, not an optimal teaching dose or evidence of mastery |

Detailed accepted, qualified and rejected proposals remain in the archived framework_refinements.md and complete_conceptual_artefacts_refined.md. They are not rewritten as though the original framework already specified the refined implementation.

## Requirement conclusions

RQ1 has a reasoned terminology/context/language basis. RQ2 has a reasoned core-explanation/scaffolding basis. RQ3 has a reasoned response/adaptation basis. Educational effectiveness, accurate diagnosis, universal content quality and uniquely necessary stage order remain unestablished.

E055–E056 identify the historical synthesis and refinement records. The [design evaluation](../03_results/results_interpretation.md) assesses the refined application separately.

### Triangulation Decision Rules

The following interpretations are used.

### Supported Across Methods

Three or four methods independently support the same conclusion.

### Strongly Supported

Evidence converges strongly and no method provides a substantive contradiction.

### Partially Supported

The general claim is supported, but some detail remains uncertain or method-dependent.

### Mixed Evidence

Methods provide materially different interpretations.

### Unresolved

The current evidence cannot determine the issue.

### Not Supported

A proposed concern or change is not supported when compared across the other methods.

## Reasons for accepting, rejecting or deferring refinements

### Rejected Refinement — Merge Stage 4 and Stage 5

### Decision

> **Rejected**

### Rationale

The SLR, informed argument, and scenario evaluation all support separate responsibilities for:

- conceptual explanation;
- structured learning support.

The correct response to the overlap concern is:

> clarify the boundary

not:

> remove one stage.

---

### Rejected Refinement — Add an Eighth Stage

### Proposed Stage

Possible suggestions included:

- Evaluate Learner Response;
- Diagnose Learner Need.

### Decision

> **Rejected**

### Rationale

The decision responsibility can be handled within Stage 7.

Adding another stage would:

- increase complexity;
- duplicate existing responsibilities;
- have no strong support from the four evaluation methods.

---

### Rejected Refinement — Add Objective Assessment or Quiz Engine

### Decision

> **Rejected**

### Rationale

The current research examines:

- terminology support;
- conceptual explanation;
- structured and adaptive scaffolding.

Objective testing, knowledge tracing, or formal mastery measurement would materially expand the project scope.

The correct response to the self-report limitation is:

> limit claims

not:

> add an entire assessment subsystem.

---

### Rejected Refinement — Add a Deterministic Language Algorithm

### Decision

> **Rejected**

### Rationale

The literature and evaluation support conceptual language-selection factors but do not establish one deterministic decision algorithm.

The framework should remain:

- conceptual;
- theory-informed;
- implementation-independent.

---

### Rejected Refinement — Permanent Stage 2 → Stage 1 Loop

### Decision

> **Rejected as the normal process**

### Rationale

The standard conceptual sequence remains coherent:

```text
Identify terminology
→ Interpret context
```

A reverse relationship is only needed where context exposes ambiguity.

Therefore, ambiguity correction is sufficient.

---

### Rejected Refinement — Major Structural Redesign

### Decision

> **Rejected**

### Rationale

The triangulated evidence shows that:

- all RQs are covered;
- every stage has a defensible purpose;
- no missing core stage was identified;
- the Photosynthesis scenario instantiates all seven stages;
- the literature supports all major capabilities;
- informed argument supports the overall sequence.

The issues found concern:

- clarity;
- decision boundaries;
- claim discipline.

They do not justify replacing the architecture.

### Deferred Issues

Some issues should be evaluated during the **design artefact evaluation** rather than solved conceptually.

> acknowledge limitation.

### D2 — Correctness of Language Selection

Conceptual framework decision:

> state conceptual selection factors.

Design evaluation task:

> test bilingual terminology behaviour across multiple STEM examples.

---

### D3 — Adaptation Consistency

Conceptual framework decision:

> clarify Stage 7 decision principles.

Design evaluation task:

> test High / Medium / Needs Support paths and first/second adaptation rounds.

---

### D4 — Fading Behaviour

Conceptual framework decision:

> retain fading theoretically.

Design evaluation task:

> test whether the implemented system reduces/concludes support appropriately under specified conditions.

---

### D5 — Cross-Domain Generalization

Conceptual framework decision:

> no structural change.

Design evaluation task:

> use simulation cases across Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, and Engineering.
